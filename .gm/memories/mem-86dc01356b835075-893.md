## Looper clip tempo sync = VARISPEED (resample), NOT time-stretch (load-bearing)

Link tempo sync changes loop PITCH (simplest time-stretch). Per-clip RubberBandWrapper is UNWIRED/vestigial. loopClip::setTempoRatio(ratio) ACCUMULATES into m_playRate (*=ratio); loopClipUpdate.cpp reads the clip FRACTIONALLY at m_playRate (linear interp over contiguous m_buffer) when m_playRate!=1 && CS_PLAYING. External tempo HALVING (masterLoopBlocks doubles -> setTempoRatio(0.5) -> m_playRate=0.5) plays loop at half speed AND half pitch (octave down) over 2x time, locked to the 2x grid. m_playRate=nativeBlocks/currentBlocks. m_playPos advances one block/update unconditionally (tracks while muted), wraps at loop end, synced to block head while rate==1. At rate==1 byte-identical (varispeed branch off). Gated to CS_PLAYING. init/_startRecording reset rate=1/pos=0. See [[looper-clip-state-machine]].
