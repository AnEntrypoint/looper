## Looper sampler = independent hold-to-record sampler (buttons 65/66, patches/sampler.h)

pSampler is a sound SOURCE not a looper. Mixes into m_input_buffer at TOP of loopMachine::update (BEFORE loop-fold/pitch/effects/microrepeat/filter) so sampler audio gets ALL effects and is recordable into a loop. Touches NO loopMachine/clip/masterPhase state. Capture reads DRY mic snapshot taken BEFORE renderInto (captureBlock before renderInto) so a recording sample never records itself or the loops. Button 65 (0x41) HELD records ONE shared chromatic sample, auto-clip silence, 25 keys play pitched (rate=2^((note-60)/12), C4=60=original). Button 66 (0x42) HELD = drum mode: hold a key records into THAT key's drum slot (keyIndex=note-48, 25 slots), plays at original pitch one-shot OVERRIDES chromatic. Keyboard routing: when sampler has content, ch1 note routes to sampler (live-pitch keyboard suppressed; modwheel/CC52 still reach pitch); no content falls through to live pitch. Cross-core: handleMidi (ISR) pushEvent into SPSC ring, Core1 drains at top of renderInto. Buffers s16 heap (~10.5MB). 16 voices oldest-steal, click-free ramps. :4445 TIME sampRec/drumMode/sampLen/drumLoaded/voices. scripts/test-sampler.cpp ALL PASS. See [[looper-grid-presets-ui]].
