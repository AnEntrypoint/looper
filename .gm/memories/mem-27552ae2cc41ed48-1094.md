## Looper continuous USB ring-WAV recorder (usbWavRecorder.{h,cpp})

A USB flash drive/HDD plugged into the Pi auto-records the wet mix to one ring-buffer WAV. Factory binds CUSBBulkOnlyMassStorageDevice (umsd1); FatFs mounts it as USB: volume (FF_VOLUME_STRS idx2 -> diskio pdrv2; SD: is emmc1). usbWavTick() (coreControlPlaneTick, Core2) probes ~1Hz f_mount(USB:), creates USB:/looper-rec.wav sized to free space (-32MB margin, <4GB FAT32, block-aligned). Streams the always-on continuousBuffer (WET engine mix, same tap loops record from): drains cb blocks (g_cbWriteBlock monotonic, cbCopyRange) in 16KB chunks (UWAV_CHUNK_BLOCKS=64), burst-cap 8 chunks/tick. ALL FatFs I/O on Core2 NEVER audio ISR (cb 180s decouples; overrun guard fast-forwards read cursor). f_sync ~0.5Hz. Ring = file offset wraps in place at ring end (f_lseek to offset 44, g_uwavWraps++). 44-byte PCM header (AUDIO_SAMPLE_RATE, 2ch, s16). :4445 UWAV verb (mounted/bytes/maxData/wraps). HW-VALIDATION PENDING (mass-storage mount+throughput not host-provable). See [[looper-audio-architecture]] [[looper-build-caveats]].
