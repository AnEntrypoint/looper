## Transpose ~4ms latency RESTORED (commit 466cdf7) — was a regression, NOT inherent

Live-pitch -12 (EngineSoladSnac PSOLA, soladSnacOctaver.h) latency had regressed to 22-53ms; user budget is NON-NEGOTIABLE ~4ms. ROOT CAUSE = an earlier buzz-mitigation traded the budget away, two coupled knobs:
(1) m_respliceFrac raised 1->8 to cut splice rate (the ~55/s amplitude-dip buzz on noisy Pi input). That let the reader gap sawtooth to initialReadOffset+per*8. RESTORED to 1 (tight ~1-period gap = PSOLA minimum). The buzz is now fixed at source by the integer-period value+slope matched splice in triggerSpliceByPeriod (commit 29b131f) so frequent resplicing is seamless. setRespliceFrac clamp floor was 2.0 (SILENTLY masked sub-1 values!) -> 0.25. frac<1 is WRONG: a sub-period drift forced through a >=1-period forward jump overshoots and lands off-phase (perr~32, eff still 0.5 but each splice off-phase).
(2) INITIAL_READ_OFFSET_DEFAULT 192->64. The 192 was 4ms of REDUNDANT headroom stacked ON TOP of the irreducible 1-period PSOLA reader lag (at downshift the reader MUST lag the writer; the lag IS the pitch shift). 64=1.3ms is well above the 16-tap sinc margin (SINC_HALF+2=10).
Total downshift latency = INITIAL_READ_OFFSET + ~1 period. Host-validated (scripts/test-transpose-latency.cpp, scale=0.5, 0.003 gaussian-noise Pi emulation, ALL PASS): 220Hz=3.6ms (<=4.5ms assert), 330Hz=4.3ms, 110Hz=5.9ms, 82Hz low-E=7.4ms (the 1-period floor, the hard PSOLA limit). eff=0.5000 perr=0 maxStep<=0.013 emerg=0. The low-E ~7ms cannot be beaten without a different algorithm. Witness gap live via gapNow() / the eng telemetry line.
