## Resolved mutable: 505-start-stop-grid-rule

Design (505-like, from user spec + masterPhase phrase-0 anchor): quant grid = powers of two of M. Sub-phrase: lengths M/2,M/4,M/8 (>=CROSSFADE*2 floor); multiple: M,2M,4M. The clip RECORDS from press (backdated) and on stop computes length L = nearest power-of-two division/multiple of M to the recorded block count (_calcQuantizeTarget already does {M/8..8M}). The START point is then anchored: recordStartPhaseOffset = the largest multiple of L that is <= the backdated-press masterPhase (i.e. floor the press phase to the L-grid), because L divides M (sub-phrase) or M divides L (multiple) so the L-grid is coherent with phrase 0. Playback play_block=(masterPhase-offset)%L then has phase 0 exactly at every L-boundary, which coincides with phrase boundaries (sub-phrase: L divides M; multiple: starts at phrase 0). Consecutive loops thus ALWAYS share the first-loop start. The latch should NOT pre-gate on a beat finer than L; instead start recording promptly (backdated) and let the stop-time length+start-floor place it on grid - matching 505 'forgiving' behavior. This unifies all four touchpoints on the M-power-of-two grid.
