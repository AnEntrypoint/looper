## Resolved mutable: led-cache-invalidated-on-reconnect

usbMidi.cpp:37 calls pTheAPC->invalidateLedCache() when a NEW MIDI device registers in usbMidiProcess. PARTIAL: gated by `if (s_registered[i]) continue;` (usbMidi.cpp:28) and s_registered[] is never cleared on disconnect, so a re-enumeration into an already-registered slot would NOT re-invalidate. On first connect the cache IS invalidated (correct). Resolved as: invalidation exists on fresh connect; the disconnect-reregister gap is captured by PRD led-cache-invalidate-on-reconnect for hardening.
