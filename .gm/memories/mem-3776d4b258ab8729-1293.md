## Looper timing: latency-backdated record + continuous buffer + Link train-on-first-loop

continuousBuffer.{h,cpp}: 180s always-on stereo rolling ring (g_cbBuffer, CB_RING_BLOCKS=180*INTEGRAL_BLOCKS_PER_SECOND). cbWriteBlock(m_input_buffer) called EVERY block from loopMachine::update AFTER live-pitch+effects so loops record the WET signal. Backdate compensates processing latency: g_cbExtraLagSamples = RubberBandWrapper::latencySamples() when transpose engaged, 0 when off. Press timestamped at MIDI ISR (apcKey25::_queueCmd press_ticks=CTimer::GetClockTicks), drained to g_pendingPressTicks before pTheLooper->command(). cbBackdatedBlock(press_ticks)=g_cbWriteBlock-backBlocks, backBlocks=(CB_FIXED_LAG_SAMPLES=96 + (now-press)us->samples)/AUDIO_BLOCK_SAMPLES, clamped. Clip records by memcpy from cbBlockPtr(m_recStartBlock+m_record_block). FIRST loop starts IMMEDIATELY on press (backdates). CONSECUTIVE loop fired by pending-record LATCH at beat downbeat (m_masterPhase%gridStep==0), m_recStartBlock=cbBackdatedBlock(0) (fixed-lag-only, NO press-backdate, NO re-snap; g_pendingPressTicks is stale at latch).
