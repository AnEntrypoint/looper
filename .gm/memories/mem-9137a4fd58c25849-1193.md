## esp32 'ticker' does Link discovery+timeline but NO ping/pong measurement -> tempo-only sync (abletonLink.cpp republishTimeline)

The esp32 'ticker' AP serves Ableton Link discovery (multicast ALIVE + tmln timeline broadcast) and DHCP, but runs NO PingResponder: it never replies to our measurement PINGs and never pings us. LIVE witness: :4445 LMSG lastMeasType=-1 (no measurement frame EVER), LINK pingsTx>5000 pongsRx=0 pingsRx=0 across fresh joins + esp32 restarts. NOT a firmware bug: discovery is bidirectional, our ports are self-consistent (OUR_MEP4_PORT==LINK_PORT==20808), PING is sourced from our advertised mep4. The esp32 simply has no responder. FIX (correct degraded mode): republishTimeline adopts an unmeasured owner's TEMPO (tempo needs no clock offset) and sets s_synced=true, but keeps s_phaseValid=false (the ghost/beat-phase offset is unknowable without measurement, so loopMachine's first-loop phase-align is skipped rather than locking to a bogus phase). Result LIVE: WLAN=joined-ticker dhcpOK=1 link=synced bpm=150 (adopted esp32 tempo) phaseValid=0. Full measurement path stays intact for peers that DO pong (Ableton Live). See [[wlan-station-dhcp-broadcast-flag]].
