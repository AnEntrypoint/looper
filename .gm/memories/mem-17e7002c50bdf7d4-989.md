## Resolved mutable: sampler-trim-quality

sampler.h _autoTrim (shared, called for chromatic AND drum via _stopRecord): threshold scan |max(L,R)|>TRIM_THRESH=200 finds first/last non-silent, memmoves leading silence out, then applies EDGE_FADE=64 fade-in AND fade-out at the trimmed edges. PROBLEM for 'proper' trimming: the EDGE_FADE FADE-IN softens a transient onset (drum hit / pluck) right after the trim -- exactly the attack you want to keep -> the trimmed sample loses its punch. And TRIM_THRESH=200 (~-44dBFS on s16) can leave low-level hum (not trimmed) or clip a soft onset. FIX: detect the onset edge with a small pre-roll (start a few samples BEFORE the threshold crossing so the attack transient is included), drop the leading fade-in (or make it very short, e.g. 4-8 samples just for declick) so the attack is preserved; keep the trailing fade-out. Optionally a slightly lower threshold + pre-roll. Witness: sampler.h _autoTrim:264-288 (EDGE_FADE leading fade + TRIM_THRESH).
